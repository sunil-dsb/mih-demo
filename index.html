Principles

When a choice is unclear, the rule higher on this list wins.

  • Readable in direct sun. Text 7:1 or more on every surface. Meaningful icons, and any edge that is the only thing showing a control (inputs, outline buttons, radios, checkboxes, chips), 4.5:1 or more. Tiles (choice, day, slot, capture) are identified by their label and carry a faint decorative edge, which WCAG allows. Glare lowers the contrast a screen actually shows, so we start high.
  • One screen, one job. One question or one decision per screen, one navy button in the thumb zone. Step flows hide the tab bar.
  • Loudness matches urgency. Everyday states are calm (grey badge, coloured dot, the word). Something that needs action gets a tint and an icon. Only Urgent (amber) and Emergency (red) get a solid colour block.
  • Money is always itemised. Labor and materials are separate, each line shows quantity × rate, and any change before the job starts shows the old value, the new value and the pro's reason.
  • Same words everywhere. The two roles are customer and pro. Pros call their customers clients (the Clients tab, "Client supplies"). Money from a pro is a quote on a public job and an estimate when a pro sends one to a client; the AI's first number is the instant quote. Both apps end their tabs with Menu.
  • Plain and real. White page, flat grey cards, no gradients, glows, card shadows, sparkle icons or stock illustrations. Words and an icon on every status, never colour alone.

Colour

Five families generated in OKLCH, each shade pinned to a contrast target against white, so the same number behaves the same in every family. Coloured text uses shade 800; icons and control edges use 600–700; tints are 50–100.

Type

The rule: size = 17 × 1.15ⁿ

Base 17 is Apple's default Body size (Android's is 16), chosen for a 30–80 audience. The ×1.15 multiplier keeps headings phone-sized: the scale lands on Apple's Subhead (15), Body (17), Title 3 (20), Title 2 (22) and Large Title (34). Line heights are ratios, so they grow with the text. Sizes are in pt on iOS and sp on Android and follow the phone's text-size setting; the largest styles stop growing at the cap shown, so a price never runs off a 360-wide screen.

Font: Inter

Inter (free, OFL) in 400–800. Of the US service apps whose fonts we could verify, Jobber and TaskRabbit use Inter; Housecall Pro uses Plus Jakarta Sans; Thumbtack and Angi use their own licensed fonts. Inter has the tallest x-height of the three free options below, a distinct 1 / l / I and true tabular figures, which matter for prices. Fallbacks: SF Pro on iOS, Roboto on Android. Bundle the four font files (about 100 KB) so both apps look the same.

Spacing, radius and size

An 8-point grid with 4 and 12 for tight spots. Inner gaps are always smaller than outer gaps. Screen margin 20, card padding 20, 32 between sections, 12 between neighbouring tap targets.
